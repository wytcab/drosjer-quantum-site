Methodology

Six factors compose the risk score; higher normalized values mean more risk. A degraded factor (stale or missing input) is dropped and the surviving weights renormalize — its neutral prior is never folded into the mean. Confidence is a separate axis from the risk grade.

orbital_regime 0.15 · conjunction_frequency 0.25 · space_weather 0.10 · component_age 0.15 · operator_track_record 0.15 · element_set_quality 0.20
